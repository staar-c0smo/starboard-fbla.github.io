Help & About

About This Platform

Byte-Sized Business Boost is a community-driven platform designed to help users discover and support small, local businesses in their area. Our goal is to elevate high-quality local businesses by providing an easy and fair way for users to explore categories, see deals, leave reviews, and bookmark their favorites.

How Our Platform Supports Small Businesses

This program directly addresses the challenge of helping local businesses gain visibility by offering the following features:

  • ✔ Category Sorting — Browse businesses by food, retail, tech, and more.
  • ✔ Reviews & Ratings — Leave trustworthy, detailed reviews that help highlight quality businesses.
  • ✔ Sort by Rating — Users can sort businesses by highest-rated to find the best local options.
  • ✔ Favorites/Bookmarks — Save businesses you want to revisit.
  • ✔ Deals & Coupons — Businesses can display special offers to attract new customers.
  • ✔ Bot Verification — Only logged-in users can leave reviews or favorite businesses, preventing spam and fake data.

These tools help local businesses grow by increasing exposure, encouraging engagement, and promoting high-quality service.

How to Use the Platform

Follow these steps to take full advantage of the features:

  1. Browse businesses on the Businesses page.
  2. Filter by category (food, retail, tech, or favorites).
  3. Sort by rating or alphabetically.
  4. Click a business to view deals, rating, and reviews.
  5. Log in to:
    • Leave a review
    • Submit a star rating
    • Bookmark a business
  6. Use your saved favorites to revisit places you love.

Why We Require Login for Reviews

To prevent bot activity and protect the integrity of ratings, users must be logged in before submitting a review or favoriting a business. This ensures:

  • Ratings are tied to real people
  • One account = one opinion, preventing spam
  • Fair visibility for small businesses
